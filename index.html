Hindsight Journal: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Applies to the Hindsight Journal app for iPhone

In short: your diary stays on your phone. Your entries, videos, photos, voice notes, goals and reflections are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The only data that leaves the device is what Apple and our subscription provider need to process and verify a purchase. It is linked to an anonymous ID, not to you.

Who we are

Hindsight Journal is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for this app and this policy. For any privacy question or request, email azpiazujavier@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Hindsight Journal is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. That covers entries, video, photo and audio recordings, text, moods, decisions and predictions, reviews, lessons, goals, check-ins and settings. We have no server that receives this content and we cannot see it.

Your device’s own backups (such as iCloud Backup) may include the app’s data, depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

If you use Backup & export, the app creates a file on your device and hands it to the share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, AirDrop, email and so on) is entirely your choice.

Permissions

The app asks for the camera, microphone and photo library only so you can record or import entries. It asks for notifications to remind you about your diary and goals. These reminders are scheduled on your device; we do not use push servers. You can change any permission in your device’s Settings.

Purchases (Hindsight Plus)

Hindsight Plus is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your name, email, card details or Apple ID.

To check whether you have an active subscription or purchase, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device, and
  • your App Store purchase and subscription history for this app (products, dates, renewal and trial status).

We have turned off RevenueCat’s collection of device identifiers and diagnostics. We do not send RevenueCat your name, email, diary content or any other attributes. This purchase data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy).

We use this anonymous purchase data for two things: to unlock what you paid for, and to see aggregate subscription statistics in RevenueCat’s dashboard (for example, how many trials become subscriptions). Neither involves your diary.

Legal basis (UK GDPR / EU GDPR): unlocking your purchase is necessary to perform our contract with you; aggregate subscription statistics rely on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No AI or cloud processing of your diary content.

Retention and deletion

Your diary data exists only on your device. Deleting an entry or goal in the app removes it, and deleting the app removes all of its data from the device (subject to your own device backups). Anonymous purchase records are kept by RevenueCat and Apple as long as needed to provide your purchase and meet legal obligations. To request deletion of the purchase record linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the address above.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data-protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or port personal data we hold, and object to or restrict processing. Because we do not hold your diary content, most requests are fully under your control in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data-protection authority.

Children

Hindsight Journal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
